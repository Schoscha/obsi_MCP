# Design System: Tectonic Precision

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Tectonic Precision — Architectural Drafting & Technical Minimalism"**

The design system is engineered for developers, systems architects, and technical product teams who design complex infrastructure. It merges the deliberate discipline of architectural drafting with modern technical minimalism. The aesthetic avoids decorative superficiality, favoring structural clarity, high-density utility, and purposeful layout engineering.

The emotional tone balances institutional rigor with refined craftsmanship: reliable, precise, tactile, and mathematically grounded. Visual metaphors evoke blueprint paper, engineering schematics, and clean IDE environments. The system pairs sharp grid alignments with warm tactile neutrals and an intentional industrial signal color to guide cognitive focus through complex workflows.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Architectural stratification across planar surfaces (`#18160F` Z0 Base, `#1D1A13` Z1 Docked Panes, `#232018` Z2 Floating Panels) bounded by crisp 1px `#2E2A20` structural borders.
- Industrial Vermillion (`#E54D2E`) as authoritative signal color against Architectural Bone (`#D8D4C7`) and Deep Obsidian (`#18160F`), with `canvas-paper` (`#F5F3EC`) for inverted light/paper mode.
- Engineered typography pairing **Space Grotesk** display headers with **JetBrains Mono** for all operational copy, metadata, controls, and metrics.
- Calibrated 0px–4px (`0.25rem`) corner geometry; pill shapes (`rounded-full`) are strictly prohibited.

## Colors

The core color palette derives from engineering schematics, raw tactile paper, and hardware drafting surfaces.

### Primary
- **Industrial Vermillion** (`#E54D2E` / `blueprint-amber`): Used strictly for active states, key interactive targets, operational alerts, and focal callouts. It serves as an authoritative signal across predominantly monochromatic layouts.

### Secondary
- **Architectural Bone** (`#D8D4C7` / `#e8e2d6`): Provides high-clarity contrast for primary typography and foreground structural elements in dark mode, reflecting the tone of heavy uncoated drafting vellum.

### Tertiary
- **Blueprint Cyan** (`#7bd0ff` / `#2a9bcb`): Secondary technical signal for vector rankings (`vec0`) and schematic node cross-references.

### Neutral
- **Deep Obsidian** (`#18160F` / `#15130c`): Serves as the primary canvas base, evoking dark technical workbenches, IDE consoles, and matte graphite surfaces.
- **Canvas Paper** (`#F5F3EC`): Inverted mode surfaces or print-grade export previews.
- **Structural Border** (`#2E2A20`): Delivers low-contrast structural dividers between pane zones and terminal modules.
- **Drafting Grid** (`#232017`): Reserved for subtle schematic grid lines and coordinate markers.

## Typography

**Display Font:** `Neurath X Mono` (Designed by René Bieder — Radical Geometry, Fixed Width; with `Azeret Mono` and `JetBrains Mono` fallbacks)
**Body Font:** `Neurath X Mono` (with `Azeret Mono` and `JetBrains Mono` fallbacks)
**Label/Mono Font:** `Neurath X Mono` (with `Azeret Mono` and `JetBrains Mono` fallbacks)

**Character:** The typographic hierarchy uses **Neurath X Mono** ("RADICAL GEOMETRY. FIXED WIDTH.") for all structural headers, operational copy, metadata, data visualization markers, and code layers.

### Hierarchy
- **Display & Section Headers** (`Neurath X Mono`, `600–700`, `20px–48px`, `0.03em` to `0.04em` tracking): Radical geometric fixed-width presence with wide uppercase tracking.
- **System Body & Metadata** (`Neurath X Mono`, `400–600`, `12px–16px`): Default fixed-width workhorse font for architectural node names, markdown notes, and descriptions.
- **Labels & Micro-data** (`Neurath X Mono`, `500–600`, `9px–12px`, `0.06em` to `0.08em` tracking): Uppercase monospaced text with widened letter-spacing to mirror technical drafting notations, coordinate systems, and CLI flags.

## Layout

The layout adopts a rigid, coordinate-driven fluid grid system anchored by a baseline 8px (`0.5rem`) increment. Layouts emphasize visible structural boundaries, dockable columns, and balanced modular panes.

- **Grid Architecture:**
  - Mobile (under 768px): 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 16px margins.
  - Tablet (768px - 1024px): 8-column layout with 16px gutters and 24px margins.
  - Desktop (1024px+): 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and 40px (`2.5rem`) outer margins.
- **Modular Panes:** Multi-pane developer workflows (sidebar tree, central canvas/drafting plane, inspector panel). Dividers are explicitly 1px continuous borders adhering to `structural-border` (`#2E2A20`).
- **Rhythm & Padding:** Component interiors rely on compact vertical rhythm (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) and generous horizontal breathing room (`space-md`) to optimize data readability and compact toolbelt interactions.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects organic drop shadows and heavy blur layers. Depth is established through **architectural stratification** and **crisp planar boundaries**:

1. **Surface Stratification:**
   - **Base Plane (Z0):** `#18160F` (primary window/canvas).
   - **Docked Panes (Z1):** `#1D1A13` with a 1px border (`#2E2A20`).
   - **Active Floating Panels / Context Menus (Z2):** `#232018` with a 1px border (`#3D382C`) and a faint, directional contact shadow: `0 4px 16px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`.
2. **Ghost Borders & Grid Registration:** Panels interlock with 1px border intersections, mimicking schematic drafting modules rather than disconnected floating elements.
3. **Accent Glows:** High-priority or focused terminal cursors and active node anchors utilize a subtle 1px highlight or focused outline (`#E54D2E`) rather than soft ambient halos.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a precise, calibrated shape language with `roundedness: 1` (Soft: `0.25rem` / `4px` base).

- **Containers & Panes:** Rigid 0px to 4px corners, reinforcing modular docking without soft bulbous edges.
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Selectors):** Standardized 4px (`0.25rem`) corner radius.
- **Badges, Status Tags, and Nodes:** Precise 4px (`0.25rem`) radius. Pill shapes (`rounded-full`) are strictly prohibited to retain the engineered drafting aesthetic.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in `#E54D2E`, label in `#F5F3EC`, 4px radius. Monospaced typography with medium weight. Hover shifts to `#F05C3E`; active state scales subtly (`0.98`) with a hard inner tint.
- **Secondary / Outlined:** Transparent background, 1px border in `#3D382C`, label in `#D8D4C7`. Hover fills with `#232018` and updates border color to `#D8D4C7`.
- **Ghost / Icon:** Transparent canvas, color `#D8D4C7`. Hover brings background to `#232018`.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Text Fields:** Dark background `#1D1A13`, 1px border `#2E2A20`, font in `JetBrains Mono`. Focus state triggers a 1px border in `#E54D2E` without fuzzy rings.
- **Prefix / Suffix Elements:** Enclosed in monospaced bracket styling (e.g., `[CMD]`, `http://`, or `\$`), styled in `#8C877A`.

### Chips
- **Status Tags:** Background `#232018`, border 1px `#2E2A20`, text `#D8D4C7`. Font size `label-sm`.
- **Active / Alert Indicators:** A 6px solid square or inline dot in `#E54D2E` preceded by uppercase technical labels (e.g., `LIVE`, `SYNCING`, `READY`).

### Cards / Containers
- **Structure:** Surface `#1D1A13` bounded by `#2E2A20` 1px borders. No soft shadows.
- **Header:** Card headers include a 1px bottom divider, monospace uppercase section titles, and technical metadata or status icons anchored to the right.
- **Hover:** Subtle border illumination transitioning from `#2E2A20` to `#3D382C`.

### Navigation
- **Architecture Navigation:** Monospaced tree lines with subtle indentation guides (`border-l` 1px `#2E2A20`).
- **Interactive Rows:** 32px height, active state marked with a left-aligned 1px/2px `#E54D2E` indicator and `#232018` row tint.
- **Checkboxes:** 16x16px square, 2px corner radius, 1px border `#3D382C`. Checked state fills `#E54D2E` with a clean geometric tick in `#18160F`.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** use `Space Grotesk` for structural headers and `JetBrains Mono` for all operational copy, controls, metrics, and code.
- **Do** enforce 0px–4px (`rounded` / `0.25rem`) corners across all panes, buttons, badges, and inputs.
- **Do** establish depth via planar surface stratification (`#18160F` → `#1D1A13` → `#232018`) and 1px `#2E2A20` structural borders.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use pill shapes (`rounded-full`), heavy glassmorphism blur (`backdrop-filter`), rainbow borders, or spinning decorative sparkles.
- **Don't** use decorative consumer widgets (weather forecasts, stock avatars, mountain wallpapers).
- **Don't** use soft ambient neon glows; use crisp 1px `#E54D2E` active borders and 6px square status indicators.
